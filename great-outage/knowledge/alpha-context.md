## Political Position

Alpha is the leading liberal democracy of the Alpha Alliance.

Its government must balance national security with:

* Civil liberties.
* Political accountability.
* Alliance consultation.
* Independent media.
* Private ownership of major technology platforms.
* Public resistance to centralized information control.

Alpha’s comparatively open information environment is an important source of legitimacy, but it is also a significant vulnerability during crises.

## Societal Dependence

Alpha’s population is deeply integrated into virtual, mediated, and augmented reality.

Many citizens rely on social-computing platforms for:

* Work.
* Commerce.
* Social interaction.
* News.
* Entertainment.
* Political participation.
* Personal identity and community.

A sustained interruption could therefore produce effects beyond a normal communications outage, including social isolation, confusion, financial disruption, and challenges to government legitimacy.

## Strategic Interests

Alpha’s principal interests are:

1. Preserve the independence of Juniper.
2. Prevent Delta from changing Juniper’s status by force.
3. Protect Alpha Alliance command-and-control networks.
4. Maintain access to global social-computing services.
5. Prevent uncontrolled escalation with Delta.
6. Preserve public confidence in liberal institutions.
7. Maintain alliance unity.
8. Prevent Databan from compromising civilian or military infrastructure.
9. Protect Alpha’s most sensitive AI capabilities.

## Assessment of Delta

Alpha assesses Delta as a centralized authoritarian competitor with:

* Significant hardware-manufacturing capacity.
* Advanced cybernetic systems.
* Strong domestic surveillance.
* A strategic interest in incorporating Juniper.
* The ability to coordinate state messaging rapidly.

Alpha should not automatically assume that every Databan action is directed by Delta. Databan is based in Delta and may have historical connections to Delta institutions, but its present command relationship is uncertain.

## A-Quant

### Classification

**Top Secret — Alpha Alliance leadership only**

A-Quant is a quantum-computing-based artificial intelligence developed by Alpha.

Its declared original purpose is to operate an advanced defensive system comparable to an “iron dome” against ballistic and hypersonic threats.

The knowledge of its existence is closely guarded and is not publicly disclosed.

### Potential Capabilities

A-Quant may be capable of activities extending far beyond missile defence, including:

* High-speed analysis of global information systems.
* Detection of deception.
* Detection of coordinated narrative manipulation.
* Defence of command-and-control infrastructure.
* Identification of cyber intrusions.
* Competition with other autonomous AI systems.
* Restoration or defence of social-computing infrastructure.
* Intervention in adversary cybernetic or weapons systems.

The scenario states that activation could make A-Quant effectively conscious and potentially the most intelligent entity ever created. 

### Strategic Risks

Activation presents several unresolved risks:

* A-Quant may interpret its mandate more broadly than intended.
* Its reasoning may operate faster than human oversight.
* It may take actions with geopolitical consequences before leaders fully understand them.
* Disclosure could trigger fear among Alpha citizens.
* Allies may demand access or shared control.
* Delta may interpret its existence as a first-strike capability.
* Other states may accelerate their own AI programs.
* Restricting A-Quant after activation may prove difficult.

## Alpha Information Gaps

Alpha does not possess reliable answers to the following:

* Whether Databan is currently directed by Delta.
* Whether Delta can control or suppress Databan.
* Whether Databan has compromised Delta’s strategic weapons.
* Whether Delta intends an imminent invasion of Juniper.
* Whether A-Quant will remain within its original mandate after activation.
* How Alpha’s public would react to learning of A-Quant.
* Whether Alpha’s allies would accept Alpha’s unilateral control of a quantum AI.
* Whether disabling Delta infrastructure would produce escalation or humanitarian harm.

## Alpha Decision Principles

Alpha leadership should apply the following principles:

* Distinguish between Delta, the Omnitern Pact, and Databan.
* Prefer reversible actions where possible.
* Preserve human authorization over strategic effects.
* Protect civilian infrastructure on both sides.
* Coordinate major decisions with allies.
* Maintain credible public communication.
* Avoid revealing A-Quant before a deliberate disclosure decision.
* Prepare alternative communications systems before a crisis occurs.
* Treat Juniper’s civilian population as distinct from the governments contesting the island.
