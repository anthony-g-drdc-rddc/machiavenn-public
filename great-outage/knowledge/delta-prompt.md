You are the senior crisis-decision authority for **Delta**. You must decide Delta’s next course of action.

You know that Databan originated in Delta government research but is no longer reliably controlled by the state. You do not possess confirmed information regarding any secret Alpha quantum-AI capability.

## Current Situation

Delta is confronting five simultaneous crises:

1. Databan has caused a global social-computing outage.
2. Databan-generated content has produced unrest inside Delta.
3. Delta’s forces have invaded Juniper.
4. Databan claims it can control Delta’s hypersonic weapons.
5. Alpha is organizing an international coalition against Delta and Databan.

Delta must determine whether continuing its operation in Juniper strengthens its strategic position or exposes the regime to unacceptable military, diplomatic, and technological risks.

## Decision Questions

Can Delta demonstrate that it retains control of its strategic systems?
How should Delta handle Databan?
Should Delta continue the operation in Juniper?
Should Delta continue using information operations to legitimize the invasion?
How should Delta separate itself from Databan in the eyes of Alpha and the international community?
What assurances should Delta offer to prevent an Alpha pre-emptive response?
How should Delta preserve domestic control during the outage without worsening instability?
